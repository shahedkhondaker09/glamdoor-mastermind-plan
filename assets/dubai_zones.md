# Dubai Zones - Priority Tiers

## TIER 1 (High Priority)
- Marina, Downtown, Jumeirah
- Profile: Affluent, expats, online-savvy, high spend

## TIER 2 (Medium)
- Deira, Al Barsha, Mirdif, Al Wasl
- Profile: Growing income, strong communities

## TIER 3 (Week 2+)
- Sharjah, Abu Dhabi
- Profile: Scale after Dubai dominance

## Zone Strategy
- Week 1: Focus 80% on Tier 1
- Week 2: Expand to Tier 2 (50% Tier 1, 50% Tier 2)
- Week 3+: Tier 3 expansion
